Meet the right triangle

A right triangle has one 90° corner. Pick one of the other two corners and call its angle θ (theta). The three sides now get names relative to θ:

  • Opposite: across from θ
  • Adjacent: next to θ (not the longest side)
  • Hypotenuse: the longest side, across from 90°
sin θopposite ÷ hypotenuse0.574
cos θadjacent ÷ hypotenuse0.819
tan θopposite ÷ adjacent0.700

Slide θ and watch the ratios change. They only depend on the angle, never on the size of the triangle.

The unit circle

A circle with radius 1. Turn the radius by an angle θ. The point where it lands is (cos θ, sin θ): cosine is how far right, sine is how far up.

    How to play

    1. Pick a level. Each level has 10 questions with 4 answers. Only one is correct.
    2. Beat the clock. You have 20 seconds per question.
    3. Protect your hearts. A wrong answer or running out of time costs 1 of your 3 hearts. At 0 hearts the game is over.
    4. Score points. Each correct answer is worth +10. After every question you get a short explanation.
    5. Unlock levels. Finish with at least 70% correct to open the next level. 90% earns 3 stars.
    6. Need help? Open Learn Trigonometry for interactive lessons.

    Keyboard: 1–4 choose an answer, Enter goes to the next question, Esc closes a dialog.

    Game paused

    The timer is stopped. Your score and hearts are safe until you leave.

    Reset all progress?

    This locks Levels 2 and 3 again and deletes your best scores and stars. This cannot be undone.
